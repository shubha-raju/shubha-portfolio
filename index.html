AI-native marketing operating model

Brand rules become machine-readable, and autonomy is a dial each process earns, not a switch. The commercial model is built in: the business case, how it's funded, and how cost per asset falls as the system matures.

Proof: Developing an AI-native marketing operating model at MediaMint; led a GenAI content supply chain pilot (OpenAI + Adobe) that identified $7–8M in annual savings; built a 40% TCO-reduction value story for AI operations.
